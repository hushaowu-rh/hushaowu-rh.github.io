Quantum Mechanics and the Arithmetic Structure of the Bost–Connes System
Shaowu Hu · March 11, 2026
Under review — Commun. Math. Phys.
Starting from the arithmetic structure of the Bost–Connes (BC) system, we systematically investigate the arithmetic origins of each of the five von Neumann axioms of quantum mechanics. The main results are: (I) The Hilbert space structure is uniquely constructed from C*-positivity; (II) Self-adjointness is an intrinsic feature of the C*-algebraic framework; (III) The numerical form of the Born rule P = |cₙ|² is uniquely determined by the overlap relation between KMS states; (IV) Unitary evolution is uniquely determined by the strict monotonicity of ζ; (V) The tensor product decomposition is a direct consequence of the fundamental theorem of arithmetic. The derivation introduces no measurement, observer, or wave function collapse. Probability emerges naturally within the BC framework as an objective relational property between two KMS states.

Unification of the Four Forces from the Bost–Connes System
Shaowu Hu · March 9, 2026
Under review — Commun. Math. Phys.
We prove that the single Bost–Connes C*-algebra reveals, via two canonical projections, the structural correspondences of all four fundamental forces. (i) Algebraic projection (Chinese Remainder Theorem): The local factor (ℤ/7ℤ)* ≅ ℤ/2ℤ × ℤ/3ℤ canonically decomposes the group algebra as ℂ[(ℤ/7ℤ)*] ≅ ℂ² ⊗ ℂ³, whose local unitary group is (U(1)ₙ × SU(2) × SU(3))/ℤ₆, the gauge group of the three non-gravitational interactions. (ii) Thermodynamic projection (KMS modular flow): The Hamiltonian H|n⟩ = (log n)|n⟩ equips ℤ₋₀ with the multiplicative metric d(m,n) = |log(m/n)|; the von Mangoldt explicit formula constitutes a matter–geometry duality identity. The prime p = 7 is singled out by a uniqueness theorem: it is the smallest prime for which the multiplicative group admits a non-trivial CRT tensor-product decomposition.

Arithmetic Structure of the Standard Model Mass Spectrum and the Bost–Connes Algebra
Shaowu Hu · March 10, 2026
Under review — Phys. Rev. D
The Standard Model is experimentally extraordinarily successful. However, its fermion mass spectrum appears as intrinsic free parameters. This paper proposes an alternative route: starting from the local arithmetic structure of the Bost–Connes algebra at the prime p=7, we carry out a unified derivation of the Standard Model mass hierarchy, the three-generation structure, the decay hierarchy, and certain coupling constants. The CRT decomposition (ℤ/7ℤ)* ≅ ℤ/2ℤ × ℤ/3ℤ simultaneously encodes the particle doublet structure and the three-generation cycle; the six characters yield six classes of fermion sectors; the doublet tension |a−(7−a)|={5,3,1} accounts for the stability ordering. The leptonic Koide formula, the neutrino mass-squared difference ratio, the d-type quark mass relations, and the weak mixing angle at the GUT scale can all be derived within the same arithmetic framework. From the single input p=7, the principal hierarchical structure of the Standard Model mass spectrum can be derived step by step.

Quasi-Black Holes and the Bost–Connes System
Shaowu Hu · March 16, 2026
Under review — Phys. Rev. D
The Bost–Connes algebra is a C*-dynamical system whose partition function is the Riemann ζ function; its unique phase transition at β = 1 is a product of the thermodynamic limit N → ∞. Physical compact objects have finitely many degrees of freedom N < ∞, so their truncated partition function Zₙ(β) is analytic for all β > 0 — no pole, no sharp phase transition, no sharp horizon. We define a quasi-black hole as a finite system with N ≫ 1 and effective inverse temperature β lying in the BC crossover region. Main results: (i) the discrete entropy is S = ψ(N) = log lcm(1,...,N), with exact ζ-zero corrections; (ii) evaporation direction and rate are determined by KMS detailed balance; (iii) evaporation terminates at the stable Planck ground state N = 1. The Bekenstein–Hawking entropy and Hawking temperature are recovered as approximations in the N → ∞ continuum limit.

Arithmetic Structure of the Neutrino Mass Spectrum and the Bost–Connes Algebra
Shaowu Hu · March 29, 2026
Under review — Phys. Rev. D
The discovery of neutrino oscillations (1998) confirmed that neutrinos possess nonzero masses, yet no theory to date has derived the mass parameters from first principles. Starting from the Legendre character χ₃ of the Bost–Connes algebra, this paper provides a complete arithmetic derivation of the neutrino mass spectrum. Core results: (1) The Gauss sum τ(χ₃)=i√7 (purely imaginary) furnishes the algebraic origin for tree-level mass vanishing; (2) S₃ symmetry yields the tri-bimaximal mixing matrix; (3) Two successive breakings of S₃ yield the reactor angle θ₁₃, the mass-squared ratio R = Δm²₃₁/Δm²₂₁ = 33.0 (experiment 33.5, deviation 1.5%), and the CP-violating phase δ_CP = −90°. With only one experimental input (Δm²₃₁), Δm²₂₁ = 7.66×10⁻⁵ eV² (experiment 7.53×10⁻⁵, deviation 1.7%) emerges automatically as a prediction.

From the Integers to the Multiverse: The View from p = 7
Shaowu Hu · March 2026
Not yet submitted
The integers form the unique infinite cyclic group up to isomorphism. The Bost–Connes algebra is the quantum statistical mechanical completion of the integers, whose partition function is precisely the Riemann zeta function. The Euler product decomposition naturally decomposes the BC algebra into independent channels indexed by primes — each prime p determines a complete physical channel with its own spacetime dimension, gauge group, and fermion structure. We prove that p=7 is the unique channel possessing simultaneously four-dimensional spacetime and an SU(2)×SU(3) gauge group.

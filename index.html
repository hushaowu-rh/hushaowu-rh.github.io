Quantum Mechanics and the Arithmetic Structure of the Bost–Connes System
ShaoWu Hu · March 11, 2026
Under review — Commun. Math. Phys.
Starting from the arithmetic structure of the Bost–Connes (BC) system, we systematically investigate the arithmetic origins of each of the five von Neumann axioms of quantum mechanics. The main results are: (I) The Hilbert space structure is uniquely constructed from C*-positivity; (II) Self-adjointness is an intrinsic feature of the C*-algebraic framework; (III) The numerical form of the Born rule P = |cₙ|² is uniquely determined by the overlap relation between KMS states; (IV) Unitary evolution is uniquely determined by the strict monotonicity of ζ; (V) The tensor product decomposition is a direct consequence of the fundamental theorem of arithmetic. The derivation introduces no measurement, observer, or wave function collapse. Probability emerges naturally within the BC framework as an objective relational property between two KMS states.

Arithmetic Structure of the Three Gauge Forces in the Standard Model and the Bost–Connes System
ShaoWu Hu · May 5, 2026
Under review — J. Geom. Phys.
We show that the local factor of the Bost–Connes C*-algebra at the prime p=7 precisely describes the Standard Model gauge group G_SM = (U(1) × SU(2) × SU(3))/Z6 via the CRT decomposition, and yields the three gauge coupling constants through spectral trace ratios (after virtual-process corrections: 1/α_s = 8.49, 1/α_EM = 137.036, 1/α_W = 29.60, all within 0.1% of experiment), with the Weinberg angle sin²θ_W = 0.2315 as an internal consistency check (experiment 0.23122, deviation 0.12%). Color unobservability follows from the self-adjointness theorem of C*-algebras. The prime p=7 is the unique prime satisfying ω(p−1) ≥ 2 with maximal thermodynamic weight at the KMS phase transition.

Arithmetic Structure of the Standard Model Mass Spectrum and the Bost–Connes System
ShaoWu Hu · May 5, 2026
Under review — J. Geom. Phys.
The 12 fermion masses of the Standard Model span 12 orders of magnitude and have hitherto been treated as independent experimental inputs. Starting from the Bost–Connes system, this paper shows that the CRT decomposition of the character group (ℤ/7ℤ)* uniquely determines six fermion sectors and three generations, and provides a unified mass formula reducing all 12 masses to four Dirichlet L-function values at the critical line s = 1/2. All nine charged fermion predictions agree with experiment to within ≤2%. The neutrino masses m₁ = 5.113, m₂ = 10.01, m₃ = 50.67 meV and mass ratio R = 34.31 are new predictions of this work, with m₁ and m₂ not yet directly measured.

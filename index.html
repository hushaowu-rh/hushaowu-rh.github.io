Quasi-Black Holes and the Bost–Connes System

Under review — Phys. Rev. D

Physical compact objects have finitely many degrees of freedom N < ∞, so their truncated partition function Zₙ(β) is analytic for all β > 0 — no pole, no sharp phase transition, no sharp horizon. We define a quasi-black hole as a finite system with N ≫ 1 and effective inverse temperature β lying in the BC crossover region. The Bekenstein–Hawking entropy and Hawking temperature are recovered as approximations in the N → ∞ continuum limit.

Four forces, two projections, one algebra.

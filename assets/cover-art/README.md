# Academic cover artwork

Decorative cover artwork for the academic collection. The original nine photographic backgrounds were generated with the built-in imagegen tool; subsequent artwork and reuse are documented below.
These are visual metaphors for the paper topics, not experimental photographs or evidence.
The prompt set is recorded in `prompts.json`. No publisher logo is used.

The original background assets are 600 × 900 JPEGs. The homepage embeds each active artwork once as a data URI; list and reader covers share it. Titles remain live HTML except for the complete, user-approved Unification and Quantum Mechanics covers described below.

## Cosmology (21 October 2026)

The user selected the original Unification nebula artwork for Cosmology. `cosmology.jpg` is an unchanged copy of `unification.jpg`; the homepage embeds it as a data URI and keeps the Cosmology title in live HTML. It is decorative artwork, not observational data. The earlier vector candidate `cosmology.svg` is retained as an unused archive.

## Motion of Matter (28 October 2026)

`motion_of_matter.jpg` replaces the earlier blue particle artwork with the user-approved thin brass pendulum clock (variant A), refined with Roman numerals and restrained detailing. The vermilion arched niche and warm water reflections form one continuous scene. The approved alignment revision centers the water droplet, rebound column and concentric ripples directly below the clock and pendulum, while retaining the surrounding composition and light. The clock now reads seven o’clock: the long minute hand points to XII and the shortened hour hand points to VII. The image is decorative artwork, not experimental evidence.

The built-in imagegen result was exported as a full-size 1024 × 1536 JPEG (quality 92), without cropping or visual retouching. The homepage embeds it once as a data URI shared by the collection and reader covers. The artwork fills the 2:3 border box, preserving its full proportions without letterboxing; the standard cover frame is retained. Its live HTML title remains visible and sits slightly lower to clear the water event at narrow mobile widths.

## BC State-Space Computer (28 October 2026)

`bc_computer.jpg` is the user-approved champagne-copper refinement of the original silicon-wafer macro. It retains the fine etched surface, thin curved edge and shallow depth of field, with five small softly gleaming die chips scattered lower in the scene. The final artwork incorporates the original website cover’s larger crop and curved-edge framing. It is decorative concept artwork, not experimental evidence.

The built-in imagegen result was exported as a full-size 1024 × 1536 JPEG (quality 92), without further cropping or retouching. The homepage embeds it once as a data URI shared by the collection and reader covers. The computer cover fills its 2:3 border box at 100%, preserving the approved framing without applying the former 132% zoom again; the standard cover border remains. Its live HTML title retains the existing standard size and position; the user explicitly allowed the lettering to overlap part of the small die chips. Other paper covers and metadata are unchanged.

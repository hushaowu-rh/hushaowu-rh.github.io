The Axioms of Quantum Mechanics and the Arithmetic Structure of the Bost–Connes System

Shao-Wu Hu · Shanghai, China
First published March 11, 2026 · Version 20 · Preprint

Abstract

Starting from the finite p-adic reduction of the Bost–Connes system and the established dynamics of matter, we give a systematic account of five axioms: quantum states, composite systems, unitary evolution, observables, and Born probabilities. Complex amplitude patterns on a finite arithmetic space, together with its normalized inner product, support pure-state and density-operator descriptions. Joint configurations of the participating degrees of freedom realize the tensor structure, while self-adjoint exchanges that balance energy and group charge generate joint unitary evolution. The full joint state retains coherence among matter, background, and auxiliaries; local mixtures arise by reducing that same state. Spectral couplings correlate the components distinguished by an observable with orthogonal records. Statistical consistency determines their Born weights, while the same instrument specifies conditional states and successive event histories. Character-based matter patterns, persistent phases, and arithmetic spectral responses give these structures their physical content. The five axioms thus connect within a common process of states, interactions, and records, providing a unified description of the evolution of matter and its observable manifestations in this framework.
